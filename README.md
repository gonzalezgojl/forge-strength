# FORGE — Strength Training

FORGE is a mobile-first, installable four-session strength and hypertrophy programme. The current plan uses three complementary upper-body days and one lower-body day to leave room for cycling.

## Weekly split

- **Upper A — Chest emphasis:** barbell bench press, incline dumbbell press, chest-supported row, neutral-grip pulldown, cable lateral raise, plus a triceps/biceps antagonist superset.
- **Upper B — Back emphasis:** wide-grip seated cable row, pronated pulldown, low-to-high cable fly, reverse pec deck, plus an overhead-triceps/preacher-curl superset.
- **Upper C — Shoulders and arms:** seated dumbbell shoulder press, machine chest press, unilateral cable row, machine lateral raise, rear-delt fly, plus a biceps/triceps superset.
- **Lower — Single weekly leg session:** hack squat, Romanian deadlift, Bulgarian split squat, seated leg curl, hip thrust, leg extension, standing calf raise and cable crunch. Leg extension and cable crunch are paired to keep the workout efficient.

## Weekly working-set map

Approximate main-target working sets: chest 11; back 13; lateral delts 6; rear delts 5; front delts 3; biceps 6; triceps 6; quadriceps 7; hamstrings 5; calves 2; abdominals 2. Glutes receive approximately 2 direct hip-thrust sets plus around 4 fractional sets from squat, hinge and unilateral patterns. Compound lifts also train secondary muscles, so the direct-set figures do not represent every overlap or a precise physiological equivalent.

## Training rules

- **Effort:** most compound sets finish around 2 repetitions in reserve (RIR); isolation work generally finishes at 1–2 RIR. Failure on every set is not required.
- **Rest:** 2.5–3 minutes on the main bench, hack squat and hinge patterns; around 2 minutes on secondary compounds; 60–90 seconds on most isolations. For paired supersets, alternate the two movements and rest after the pair using the longer prescribed interval.
- **Range and execution:** use the largest comfortable, controlled range of motion. Lower the load under control for roughly 2 seconds; do not deliberately slow repetitions to an extreme.
- **Progression:** use double progression. When every working set reaches the top of its rep range at the target RIR with consistent technique, increase the load by the smallest practical increment and build back up from the bottom of the range.
- **Review:** keep the exercise menu stable for approximately 6–8 weeks. Adjust accessory sets if recovery, sleep, joint comfort or cycling performance deteriorates.
- **Schedule:** suggested spacing is Mon Upper A, Wed Upper B, Fri Upper C and Sun Lower. Keep the hardest bike ride away from the 24 hours before the Lower session; easy cycling can be used as recovery if it stays easy.

## App functions

- 28 exercise slots across four sessions, with English instructions and technique cues.
- Exercise-specific sets, reps, rest, RIR, equipment and estimated session duration.
- Supersets are visually grouped and labelled; heavy compound lifts remain straight sets.
- Exercise check-offs and completed-session history saved locally on the device.
- Two-position exercise illustrations are loaded from RepDB where matching records are available; local written instructions remain available when the image dataset is unavailable.
- No rest timers, countdowns or weight-entry fields.
- PWA manifest and service worker for Add to Home Screen.

## Evidence note

The programme is a practical starting configuration, not a universally perfect prescription. See `TRAINING-RESEARCH.md` for the studies, findings, limitations and how they informed exercise selection, weekly volume, rest periods, RIR and supersets.

## Publish

Publish the repository root through GitHub Pages. On iPhone, open the Pages URL in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Progress is stored in the current browser/device and does not sync automatically between devices. Version 4 preserves completed-session history from version 3 but starts exercise check-offs fresh because the exercise list has changed.
