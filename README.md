# FORGE — Strength Training

FORGE is a mobile-first, installable four-session strength-training plan.

## Weekly split

- **Monday — PUSH:** chest, shoulders and triceps.
- **Tuesday — PULL:** back, rear delts and biceps.
- **Thursday — UPPER:** back, chest, shoulders and an arm superset.
- **Saturday — LEGS:** one combined lower-body session.

## Exercise plan

### PUSH
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Barbell Bench Press | 2 × 5 + 1 × 9 | 3 min |
| Chest Press Machine | 2 × 9 | 2 min |
| Cable Chest Fly | 3 × 12 | 75 sec |
| Cable Lateral Raise | 3 × 9 | 60 sec |
| Smith Machine Military Press | 2 × 9 | 2 min |
| Cable Triceps Extension | 3 × 12 | 75 sec |

### PULL
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Barbell Row | 3 × 9 | 2.5–3 min |
| Close-Grip Lat Pulldown | 2 × 12 | 2 min |
| Single-Arm Cable Pullover | 3 × 9 / side | 75 sec |
| Cable Rear-Delt Fly | 3 × 12 | 75 sec |
| Hammer Curl | 3 × 9 | 75 sec |
| EZ-Bar Preacher Curl | 3 × 12 | 75 sec |

### UPPER
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown | 3 × 12 | 2 min |
| T-Bar Row | 2 × 9 | 2.5 min |
| Incline Machine Chest Press | 3 × 9 | 2 min |
| Pec Fly Machine | 2 × 12 | 75 sec |
| Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 3 × 9 | 60 sec |
| EZ-Bar Preacher Curl + EZ-Bar Skull Crusher | 3 × 12 each | Superset; 90 sec after pair |

### LEGS
| Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Barbell Squat | 2 × 5 + 1 × 9 | 3 min |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3 × 12 | 2.5 min |
| Smith Machine Bulgarian Split Squat | 2 × 9 / leg | 2 min |
| Hack Squat | 2 × 9 | 2.5 min |
| Hip Thrust | 2 × 9 | 2 min |
| Leg Extension + Leg Curl | 3 × 12 each | Superset; 90 sec after pair |

## PWA installation

Enable GitHub Pages from **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.

## Exercise illustrations

Exercise movement illustrations show the start and peak positions and are sourced from the [RepDB free exercise dataset](https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset). Attribution is shown in the app. Image assets are loaded from `exercise-dataset.com` and are not redistributed in this repository.

## Weekly tracking

Exercise checks and completed-session indicators are stored locally on the device for the current Monday–Sunday week. Completed session tabs remain green until that session or the whole week is reset. A new week starts with a clean weekly checklist; historical completion records remain stored locally.
